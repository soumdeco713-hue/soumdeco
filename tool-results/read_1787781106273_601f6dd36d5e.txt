   389→demonstrably holds, so remediation does not accidentally discard working engineering. Each item below was
   390→verified against the code during this audit, not taken from the project's own documentation.
   391→1. Read-path resilience is genuinely well engineered: the fallback chain (Worker KV, static JSON,
   392→localStorage cache, seed data) has timeouts on every fetch, per-source caches, and zero-error handling, so a
   393→visitor always gets a rendered storefront even when every backend is down.

   395→constant-time signature verification, and 8-hour expiry are all implemented properly - the problems are the
   396→leaked signing key (C-01) and the missing rate limiting (H-05), not the cryptography.

   417→The overall pattern is a codebase with two distinct personalities. The read path - the part a visitor experiences -
   418→is engineered with unusual care: layered fallbacks, timeouts on every hop, self-healing caches, and defensive
   419→parsing throughout. The write path - the part that touches money, inventory, and administration - is where
   420→nearly every finding lives: trust boundaries asserted in comments rather than enforced in code, fail-open gates,
